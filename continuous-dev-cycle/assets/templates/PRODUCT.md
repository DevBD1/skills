# Product

This is the product constitution: long-term vision, principles, identity, and governance. It changes rarely and deliberately. It does not describe the current implementation — feature status lives in `docs/sprints/` and `docs/releases/`, operational truth lives in READMEs.

## Vision

State the durable problem this product solves and for whom. One or two paragraphs.

## Identity

- **Audience:** who the product serves.
- **Positioning:** what it is and, just as importantly, what it is not.
- **Tone and voice:** how the product speaks, if user-facing.

## Governance

- **Decision making:** who decides scope, releases, and breaking changes, and how.
- **Change process:** how this document itself changes (e.g. PR with explicit approval).
- **Non-goals:** things this product deliberately will not do.
