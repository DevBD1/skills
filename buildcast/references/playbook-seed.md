# Playbook seed

Starting content for a new project's `PLAYBOOK.md`. Copy **everything below the first
horizontal rule** — including the grounding-labels legend — into the installed file
(add a title line `# <Project> — Content Playbook` and a one-line purpose note above
it). Let the project's own retros grow it: seeded rules that a retro contradicts get
updated, not defended.

---

Grounding labels:

- **[X-2026]** — traceable to X's open-sourced 2026 recommendation algorithm
  (embedding-based retrieval; ~17 positive / ~5 negative predicted-action heads where
  replies, quotes, DM-shares, dwell, and follows outweigh likes and block/mute/report
  subtract; freshness and author-diversity effects). Applies to X; often directionally
  true elsewhere.
- **[general]** — sound practice, not provable from platform code.
- **[project]** — earned from this project's own post retros; link the source entry.

---

## Writing

- **Optimize for the heavy signals, not likes.** [X-2026] Rankers predict each action
  separately; replies, quotes, DM-shares, bookmarks, dwell, and follows outweigh likes.
  Write posts that make people respond, quote, or send to a friend.
- **The first line must work for a cold reader.** [X-2026] Out-of-network viewers have
  zero context on the project; a hook that assumes they know it loses them instantly —
  and on long posts, only the first ~280 characters show before the fold, so the
  sharpest line goes there.
- **No hashtags on X.** [X-2026] Retrieval is embedding-based on the content itself;
  hashtags add nothing and read spammy. (Other platforms differ — check per platform.)
- **No engagement bait** ("RT if…", "drop a link below"). [X-2026] It raises the
  block / mute / report / not-interested probabilities the ranker subtracts for.
- **Attach visual receipts.** [general] Screenshots, before/afters, design shots — media
  holds the eye and drives replies without asking. Show the thing, don't describe it.
- **Milestones are hooks, not subordinate clauses.** [general] Lead with the reveal
  (a name, a launch, a number); the process is the payoff, not the preamble.
- **Public bets create arcs.** [general] A stated bet + deadline + success criteria
  makes the follow-up post a payoff people waited for.
- **Principled withholding beats teasing.** [general] "Not sharing it — that's the
  point" builds curiosity without bait.

## Posting mechanics

- **Post when your audience is active.** [X-2026] Freshness is structurally favored;
  early in-network engagement is what unlocks out-of-network reach.
- **Space posts 1–2 days apart.** [X-2026] Rapid-fire posting splits early engagement
  and same-author posts decay within a feed. A thread counts as one unit — thread freely.
- **Keep material in reserve.** [general] Build-in-public is an arc; don't spend every
  milestone in one post. End each post where the next one begins.

## Growth

- **Reply substantively to larger accounts in the niche.** [general] At small follower
  counts this outperforms posting — good replies drive profile clicks → follows.
- **Never do follow-for-follow.** [X-2026] Reciprocity followers don't engage, which
  fills the in-network launch pool with viewers whose predicted engagement is near zero
  and suppresses every future post. Fifty engaged followers beat five hundred
  follow-backs.

## Measurement

- Capture metrics at **~24h** and **~7d** for every post. [general]
- Judge posts by replies / reposts / bookmarks / new follows **per view** — not by
  likes. [X-2026]
